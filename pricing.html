Questions before money changes hands

Fair enough. I'd have questions too.

Do I need to book the free fit call first?

Nope. It's there if you want it. If you already know which option you want, you don't need to spend fifteen minutes proving it to me.

What if I choose the wrong package?

We'll sort it out. If it becomes obvious you need something different, we're allowed to change the plan rather than stubbornly continuing because a webpage once said so.

Do I have to use every session?

The packages have a time window so they don't drift into eternity, but we're not filling sessions with nonsense just for the sake of ticking boxes.

Where do sessions happen?

Sessions are online, so you can join from wherever you're comfortable without travelling across the country to sit opposite me in an aggressively neutral office.

Is this therapy?

No. FlamingoRise coaching is not therapy, counselling, medical care or crisis support. It's practical, conversation-based coaching focused on where you are, what isn't working and what you want to do next.

Can I buy resources without having coaching?

Absolutely. That's the whole point of The Rise Hub. Coaching isn't the compulsory entrance fee to getting something useful from FlamingoRise.
