One more boring but important bit

The Disclaimer.

What FlamingoRise is. What it isn't. No smoke machine required.

FlamingoRise exists to provide practical coaching, tools, resources and conversations that can help you think, untangle things and decide what you want to do next.

There are also some things FlamingoRise absolutely isn't, so this page makes those boundaries clear.

01

General information

The information provided through the FlamingoRise website, coaching services, member areas and digital resources is intended for general informational, educational and personal-development purposes.

Every person's circumstances are different.

Something being useful to one person does not automatically mean it will be appropriate for everybody.

Context matters. Humans are annoyingly non-standard.

02

What FlamingoRise coaching is

FlamingoRise coaching is practical, conversation-based support designed to help you explore where you are, identify what is not working, consider your options and decide what you want to do next.

Coaching may involve questions, reflection, practical exercises, resources and conversations about change, boundaries, confidence, decisions, identity, relationships, priorities and other areas of everyday life.

Coaching is collaborative.

I am not here to take control of your life or make your decisions for you.

We can examine the map together. You're still holding the steering wheel.

03

Coaching is not therapy

FlamingoRise coaching is not psychotherapy, counselling, psychiatric treatment or another clinical mental-health service.

Coaching should not be used as a replacement for professional mental-health treatment where that type of support is required.

Discussions may naturally involve difficult experiences, emotions or previous events because real life does not arrive neatly divided into labelled boxes.

That does not turn a coaching relationship into a therapeutic or clinical one.

04

Medical and health information

FlamingoRise does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment, medical advice or healthcare services.

Nothing on this website or within FlamingoRise resources should be treated as personalised medical advice.

If you have concerns about your physical or mental health, medication, treatment or symptoms, you should seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

05

Legal, financial and other professional advice

FlamingoRise does not provide legal, financial, investment, tax or other regulated professional advice.

Coaching conversations may include practical discussion about situations involving work, money, relationships, organisations or other parts of life.

That conversation is not a substitute for advice from a suitably qualified professional where specialist advice is required.

06

The Rise Hub and digital resources

FlamingoRise resources may include worksheets, planners, prompts, guides, workbooks, exercises, toolkits, bundles and other digital content.

These resources are designed to encourage reflection, organisation, exploration and practical action.

They are not diagnostic tools and do not provide individual professional advice simply because you complete them.

You are free to use the parts that are useful and leave the parts that aren't.

A worksheet is a tool. It is not the boss of you.

07

Results and outcomes

FlamingoRise does not guarantee a particular result from coaching, resources or other content.

Results can depend on many things outside FlamingoRise's control, including your circumstances, decisions, participation, timing and what happens in the rest of your life.

Sometimes progress is obvious.

Sometimes progress is realising the original plan was bollocks and making a different one.

08

Personal experiences and stories

FlamingoRise may sometimes refer to personal experiences, lived experience or real-life examples.

These are shared to provide context, perspective or illustration.

A personal experience is not evidence that another person will experience the same circumstances or outcome.

Where details have been changed, anonymised or combined to protect privacy, this may not always be separately identified unless it is relevant to understanding the content.

09

Veterans and members of the Armed Forces community

FlamingoRise may be used by veterans, serving personnel, Armed Forces families and other members of the military community.

FlamingoRise is not an emergency military welfare service, statutory service, healthcare provider or replacement for specialist veterans' services.

Where somebody requires specialist support outside the scope of FlamingoRise, another organisation or suitably qualified professional may be more appropriate.

Being part of the Armed Forces community does not mean every difficulty needs to be treated as a clinical or military problem either.

Sometimes a veteran is simply a human trying to sort some shit out. Revolutionary concept, apparently.

10

Emergency and crisis support

FlamingoRise is not an emergency service, crisis line or 24-hour support service.

Messages, emails, website forms and WhatsApp communications are not continuously monitored.

If you or another person is in immediate danger or requires urgent medical assistance, use the appropriate emergency or urgent professional service rather than waiting for a response from FlamingoRise.

11

Links and third-party services

The FlamingoRise website may contain links to other websites, platforms, organisations, resources or services.

External websites operate independently and FlamingoRise does not control their content, availability, security, privacy practices or subsequent changes.

A link does not automatically mean FlamingoRise endorses everything available on that website.

Third-party services used to operate FlamingoRise may also have their own terms and privacy policies.

12

Testimonials and other people's experiences

FlamingoRise may display testimonials, feedback or examples of experiences from people who have used its services or resources.

These represent those individuals' experiences.

They do not guarantee that everybody will receive the same result.

Where testimonials are edited for length or clarity, their meaning will not intentionally be changed.

13

Your decisions remain yours

FlamingoRise can provide questions, tools, perspectives, information and support.

You remain responsible for evaluating that information against your own circumstances and deciding what action, if any, you take.

You should obtain appropriate specialist advice before making decisions where professional advice is reasonably required.

Your life. Your decisions. Your consequences. I don't get voting rights.

14

Accuracy and availability

Reasonable efforts are made to keep FlamingoRise website information accurate and useful.

Information may become outdated, services may change and mistakes occasionally happen because apparently websites refuse to remain frozen in time.

Content may therefore be corrected, updated or removed where necessary.

15

Limitation of this disclaimer

Nothing in this disclaimer is intended to exclude or restrict rights or responsibilities that cannot legally be excluded or restricted.

Your statutory rights under applicable UK law are not removed by this disclaimer.
