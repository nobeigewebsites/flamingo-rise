The woman behind the flamingo

I didn't learn this shit from a textbook.

I lived it.

FlamingoRise wasn't born because I had life figured out. It was born because life repeatedly went off-script and somewhere along the way I learned how to keep getting back up.

yep. that's me ↑

The real version

My life has never been particularly beige.

I've lived through the kind of chapters that change you. Some gently. Some by kicking the fucking door off its hinges.

I've rebuilt, adapted, started again, questioned everything, made mistakes, changed my mind and learned that surviving something and actually living afterwards are two very different things.

And somewhere in all of that, I realised something:

People don't always need somebody to fix them. Sometimes they need somebody willing to tell them the truth while they figure out what comes next.

That's the space FlamingoRise came from.

Surviving isn't the same thing as living.

Took me a while too.

Why FlamingoRise exists

Because sometimes “just stay positive” deserves to be launched into the sea.

Real life is complicated. Healing is messy. Change isn't linear and sometimes the brave thing isn't pushing harder — it's admitting something isn't bloody working.

I wanted FlamingoRise to be somewhere you could show up exactly as you are without having to make your story prettier first.

Somewhere honest enough to challenge you, but safe enough that you don't have to defend yourself while it happens.
