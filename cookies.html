Cookie Notice

Cookies sound considerably more exciting than they are. This notice explains the cookies and similar technologies used by FlamingoRise, why they're needed and what choices you have.

This Cookie Notice applies to the FlamingoRise Coaching website and member area.

FlamingoRise does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioural tracking cookies on its own website.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. What are cookies and similar technologies?

Cookies are small pieces of information stored on your computer, phone or other device when you use a website.

Websites may also use technologies such as browser storage that perform similar functions without technically being cookies.

They can be used for things such as keeping a website working, maintaining a secure login, remembering choices or protecting accounts from misuse.

2. Essential technologies used by FlamingoRise

FlamingoRise uses essential cookies and similar technologies where they are necessary to provide website and member-area functionality.

These may be used to:

  • create and maintain secure member sessions;
  • keep members signed in;
  • authenticate account access;
  • protect accounts and services from misuse;
  • provide access to resources connected with a member account;
  • remember that you have seen the FlamingoRise cookie notice.

Technologies that are strictly necessary for a service requested by you do not generally require consent in the same way as optional advertising or analytics technologies.

3. FlamingoRise cookie notice preference

When you select “Got it” on the FlamingoRise cookie notice, the website stores a small preference in your browser so that the notice does not need to appear every time you visit.

This preference is used only to remember that the notice has already been shown.

Clearing your browser's site data may cause the notice to appear again.

4. Member accounts and Clerk

FlamingoRise uses Clerk to provide member authentication and account login.

Clerk may use cookies, browser storage or similar technologies where necessary to create and maintain authentication sessions, recognise signed-in users and protect account security.

Without these essential technologies, member login and account functionality may not work correctly.

5. Supabase and member resources

FlamingoRise uses Supabase for parts of the member-area infrastructure, including member access records and secure digital resource access.

Technical information may be processed where necessary to communicate securely with these services and provide authorised account functionality.

Private resource access is controlled through authenticated member access rather than public download links.

6. Analytics, advertising and tracking

FlamingoRise does not currently intentionally place advertising cookies, behavioural tracking cookies or optional analytics cookies on its own website.

If non-essential analytics, advertising or tracking technology is introduced in the future, this notice will be updated.

Where consent is legally required, those technologies will not be activated until an appropriate choice has been provided.

7. Payhip and purchases

FlamingoRise uses Payhip to sell paid digital products, bundles and courses.

When you follow a purchase link from FlamingoRise, you are taken to Payhip's website.

Payhip and payment providers available through Payhip may use their own cookies or similar technologies for purposes such as:

  • checkout and shopping basket functionality;
  • payment processing;
  • transaction security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • remembering preferences;
  • other purposes explained in their own privacy or cookie information.

Those services operate their own websites and control their own cookie practices.

8. Google Fonts and technical services

FlamingoRise uses third-party technical services to operate and display the website.

This currently includes Google Fonts for website typography, as well as website hosting and infrastructure providers.

These providers may receive limited technical information, such as IP address, browser information or request data, where this is necessary to deliver their service.

9. External websites

FlamingoRise may contain links to websites operated by other organisations.

Once you leave FlamingoRise, those websites may use their own cookies and similar technologies.

FlamingoRise does not control cookies placed by another organisation on its own website.

10. Managing cookies and site data

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies and other stored website data.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • delete existing cookies and site data;
  • block cookies;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • control permissions for individual websites;
  • clear stored site data when you close your browser.

Blocking or deleting essential authentication technologies may log you out or prevent parts of the FlamingoRise member area from working correctly.

11. Your choices

FlamingoRise does not treat continued browsing as consent to non-essential cookies.

The current cookie notice explains the essential technologies used by the website. The “Got it” button acknowledges that notice; it is not used as consent for advertising or behavioural tracking.

If FlamingoRise introduces non-essential technologies that require consent, visitors will be given an appropriate way to accept or refuse them.

12. Changes to this Cookie Notice

This notice may be updated if FlamingoRise changes the technology or services used on the website.

The date near the top of this page will be updated when significant changes are made.
