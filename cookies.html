Cookie Notice

Cookies sound considerably more exciting than they are. This notice explains what cookies and similar technologies are, when FlamingoRise may use them and what happens when you visit third-party services such as Payhip.

This Cookie Notice applies to the FlamingoRise Coaching website.

Third-party websites and platforms have their own cookie practices and privacy information.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. What is a cookie?

A cookie is a small piece of information stored on your computer, phone or other device when you visit a website.

Cookies can be used for things such as:

  • keeping a website working properly;
  • remembering choices or preferences;
  • keeping you signed in;
  • protecting accounts and transactions;
  • remembering items in a shopping basket;
  • measuring how a website is used;
  • advertising or tracking activity across websites.

Similar technologies can sometimes perform the same job without technically being called a cookie.

2. Essential and non-essential cookies

Some cookies are necessary for a website or online service to work properly.

These are often called strictly necessary cookies.

They may be used for things such as security, account login, fraud prevention, checkout functions or remembering something you have specifically asked the website to do.

Other cookies — such as analytics, advertising or behavioural tracking cookies — are generally considered non-essential.

Where the law requires consent for a non-essential cookie or similar technology, it should not be used until you have been given an appropriate choice.

3. Cookies on the main FlamingoRise website

The main FlamingoRise website is currently designed as a straightforward informational and resource website.

FlamingoRise does not intentionally use advertising cookies or behavioural tracking technologies on the main website.

If analytics, advertising, embedded tracking tools or other non-essential technologies are introduced in the future, this Cookie Notice will be updated and an appropriate consent mechanism will be added where required.

4. Website hosting and technical services

The FlamingoRise website is hosted using third-party technical services.

Hosting or infrastructure providers may process limited technical information necessary to deliver, secure or maintain the website.

This may include information such as IP addresses, browser details, request logs or security information.

Technical processing required simply to provide or secure a requested website may be treated differently from optional advertising or tracking technologies.

5. Payhip

FlamingoRise uses Payhip to sell and deliver paid digital products, bundles and courses.

When you click a FlamingoRise purchase link, you leave the main FlamingoRise website and visit Payhip's platform.

Payhip may use cookies or similar technologies for purposes including:

  • checkout functionality;
  • shopping baskets;
  • customer accounts;
  • member or course access;
  • security;
  • fraud prevention;
  • payment processing;
  • remembering preferences;
  • other purposes described in Payhip's own policies.

Payhip controls its own website and cookie practices. You should review the information Payhip provides when using its platform.

6. Payment providers

Payment services available through Payhip may also use cookies or similar technologies when you complete a purchase.

These technologies may be necessary for payment processing, authentication, fraud prevention and transaction security.

Payment providers operate their own systems and privacy practices.

7. External websites

FlamingoRise may contain links to websites or services operated by other organisations.

When you follow one of those links, the external website may set its own cookies.

FlamingoRise does not control cookies placed by a website after you have left the FlamingoRise website.

You should review the cookie or privacy information provided by the website you are visiting.

8. Embedded third-party content

If FlamingoRise introduces embedded content in the future — such as videos, social-media content, maps or other external tools — the provider of that content may use cookies or similar technologies.

Where those technologies are non-essential and consent is required, FlamingoRise will aim to prevent them from being activated before the appropriate choice has been made.

9. Managing cookies in your browser

Most browsers allow you to view, delete or block cookies.

Depending on your browser, you may be able to:

  • delete existing cookies;
  • block all cookies;
  • block third-party cookies;
  • allow cookies only for particular websites;
  • clear cookies automatically when you close your browser.

Blocking essential cookies may stop some online services from working correctly.

10. Cookie consent

FlamingoRise will not rely on the fact that somebody simply continued browsing as consent for non-essential cookies.

If non-essential cookies requiring consent are introduced, visitors will be given a clear way to make that choice.

Any consent mechanism introduced will also provide a way to refuse non-essential cookies.

11. Changes to this Cookie Notice

This notice may be updated if FlamingoRise changes the technology used on the website or introduces new services.

The date at the top of this page will be updated when significant changes are made.
