02

What information might be collected?

The information collected depends on how you use FlamingoRise.

This may include:

  • Your name.
  • Your email address.
  • Your telephone number if you choose to provide it.
  • Information you include in a contact enquiry.
  • Account information if you create an account.
  • Information about resources or services you purchase.
  • Information needed to provide coaching or other services you have requested.
  • Basic technical information relating to use of the website where necessary for security or operation.
  • Cookie and preference information where cookies or similar technologies are used.

FlamingoRise does not need to know everything about you simply because you use the website.

If information isn't reasonably needed, I don't particularly want to collect the bastard.

03

Why is your information used?

Personal information may be used where necessary to:

  • Respond to enquiries.
  • Arrange a fit call or coaching session.
  • Provide services you have requested.
  • Create and manage a FlamingoRise account.
  • Provide access to purchased resources.
  • Process and maintain records of purchases.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Keep the website and member areas secure.
  • Meet legal, accounting or regulatory obligations.
  • Send marketing communications where you have chosen to receive them.

The legal basis used will depend on the reason the information is being processed and may include performance of a contract, legitimate interests, legal obligations or your consent.

04

Contact forms, email, phone and WhatsApp

If you contact FlamingoRise, the information you provide will be used to respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, provide the service you've asked about.

Website contact form submissions are processed using Formspree.

If you choose to contact FlamingoRise using WhatsApp, email or telephone, your information will also be processed by the relevant communication provider.

You choose which contact method you prefer.

05

FlamingoRise accounts

If member accounts are available, information such as your first name and email address may be used to create and manage your account.

Your account may also contain information about resources you have purchased, content available to you and relevant service history.

Passwords will be handled through the secure authentication system used by FlamingoRise.

Passwords will not be collected through the ordinary FlamingoRise contact form.

06

Payments

Where FlamingoRise accepts online payments, payment information will be processed through a secure third-party payment provider.

FlamingoRise will not deliberately collect or store full payment-card details through an ordinary website form.

The name of the payment provider and relevant privacy information will be added here when the payment system is finalised.

07

Other companies involved

Sometimes another service has to process information so FlamingoRise can actually function.

These may include services used for:

  • Website hosting.
  • Contact-form processing.
  • Email and communications.
  • Secure account authentication.
  • Payment processing.
  • Digital resource delivery.
  • Website security.
  • Analytics or website performance, if these are enabled.

FlamingoRise will only share personal information where there is a legitimate reason to do so.

This section will be updated with the final providers used by the website once the technical setup is complete.

08

Cookies

The website may use cookies or similar technologies where they are required for features such as accounts, security, preferences, checkout or website operation.

Optional cookies, such as analytics or marketing cookies, will be handled according to the cookie choices available on the website where required.

A final cookie notice will be added once the website's services are complete so it describes the cookies that actually exist rather than inventing a fictional biscuit collection.

09

How long is information kept?

Personal information will not be kept indefinitely just because storage exists.

How long information is retained depends on why it was collected, including whether it is needed to provide a service, maintain purchase records, deal with an enquiry or comply with legal and accounting requirements.

Information that is no longer reasonably required will be deleted or anonymised where appropriate.

10

Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures will be used to protect personal information against unauthorised access, alteration, disclosure or loss.

Services involving passwords, payments or other sensitive account functions will use appropriate specialist providers rather than ordinary website forms.

No internet-based system can promise absolute security, but security will be considered when choosing the services used by FlamingoRise.

11

Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights relating to your personal information, including the right to:

  • Ask what personal information is held about you.
  • Ask for inaccurate information to be corrected.
  • Ask for information to be deleted in some circumstances.
  • Ask for processing to be restricted in some circumstances.
  • Object to certain uses of your information.
  • Receive certain information in a portable format where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Requests can be made using the contact details in this policy.

You also have the right to raise concerns with the UK's data protection regulator, the Information Commissioner's Office.
