Privacy Notice

Your personal information is your business. This notice explains what FlamingoRise Coaching may collect, why it is needed, how it is used and what rights you have. No mysterious data hoarding. No selling your details. No sneaky bullshit.

FlamingoRise Coaching is operated by a UK sole trader.

For questions about this Privacy Notice or your personal data, contact: flamingorisecoaching@gmail.com

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. Who is responsible for your data?

FlamingoRise Coaching is responsible for the personal information it collects and uses for its own business activities. In UK data protection terminology, this generally means FlamingoRise Coaching acts as the data controller for that information.

Other organisations used to provide services — such as payment processors, website platforms and digital delivery providers — may also process personal data under their own responsibilities and privacy terms.

2. What personal information may be collected?

Depending on how you use FlamingoRise, information may include:

  • your name;
  • your email address;
  • contact details you choose to provide;
  • information included in an enquiry or message;
  • purchase and order information;
  • the products, bundles or courses you have purchased;
  • account or membership information connected with digital products or courses;
  • country or location information where this is required for payment, tax or VAT purposes;
  • records of customer support conversations;
  • technical information generated when you use the website, where applicable, such as basic device, browser or security logs.

FlamingoRise does not intentionally ask for more information than is reasonably needed to provide its services.

3. Payment information

Paid digital products, bundles and courses are sold through Payhip. Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers made available through Payhip.

FlamingoRise Coaching does not receive or store your complete debit or credit card details. Those details are handled by the relevant payment provider.

FlamingoRise may receive information connected with your order, such as your name, email address, location information, products purchased and transaction details needed to manage the purchase and provide customer support.

4. Why your information is used

Personal information may be used to:

  • process and manage purchases;
  • deliver digital products and course access;
  • respond to enquiries;
  • provide customer support;
  • maintain purchase and transaction records;
  • deal with refunds, disputes or technical problems;
  • protect FlamingoRise, its website and its customers from fraud, misuse or security issues;
  • meet legal, accounting and tax obligations;
  • communicate important information about a product or service you have purchased;
  • send marketing communications where you have actively chosen to receive them, where applicable.

5. Lawful bases for using your information

UK data protection law requires a lawful reason for using personal information. The lawful basis depends on what the information is being used for.

Purpose Typical lawful basis
Processing purchases and providing digital products, bundles or course access Contract — the information is needed to provide what you purchased.
Responding to enquiries before you purchase Contract / steps requested before a contract where your enquiry relates to purchasing or using a service.
Customer support, security and protecting the business Legitimate interests — operating the business properly, safely and effectively.
Accounting, tax or legal record keeping Legal obligation.
Optional marketing emails Consent, where consent is required and has been given.

6. Sensitive information

Some FlamingoRise resources deal with personal topics such as emotions, trauma, relationships, grief and wellbeing.

Purchasing a resource about one of these topics does not automatically mean FlamingoRise knows anything about your personal circumstances beyond the fact that the product was purchased.

Please avoid sending highly sensitive personal, medical or confidential information by email unless it is genuinely necessary.

7. Who information may be shared with

FlamingoRise does not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with trusted service providers where this is necessary to operate FlamingoRise, including:

  • Payhip — digital storefront, order management, product delivery and customer account functions;
  • payment providers made available through Payhip, such as the payment service selected at checkout;
  • website hosting and technical service providers;
  • email providers;
  • professional advisers such as accountants or legal advisers, where necessary;
  • government bodies, regulators, courts or law enforcement where disclosure is required by law.

Service providers are only given the information reasonably required for the service they provide.

8. Payhip accounts and member areas

If you purchase a digital product or course through Payhip, Payhip may create or maintain customer account information so that you can access your purchases or member content.

Information entered directly into Payhip is also subject to Payhip's own privacy practices and systems.

FlamingoRise can access customer information made available through its Payhip creator dashboard, including details supplied during purchase or account registration, where this is needed for order management, customer support, product access, refunds or administration.

FlamingoRise does not have access to your full payment card details. Payment information is handled by the payment provider used at checkout.

9. International data transfers

Some technology providers used by FlamingoRise or Payhip may process or store information outside the UK.

Where UK data protection law requires safeguards for an international transfer, the relevant provider is expected to use an appropriate lawful transfer mechanism.

More information about how individual third-party providers handle international transfers can be found in their own privacy notices.

10. How long information is kept

Personal information is not kept forever just because it exists.

How long information is retained depends on why it is held. For example:

  • enquiry emails may be kept while the enquiry is active and for a reasonable period afterwards;
  • customer support records may be retained while they remain relevant to the purchase or issue;
  • purchase and accounting information may need to be retained for longer where this is required for tax, accounting, legal or fraud-prevention purposes;
  • account information may remain available while an account, purchase or course access remains active.

Information that is no longer reasonably required will be deleted, anonymised or securely disposed of where appropriate.

11. Your data protection rights

Depending on the circumstances, UK data protection law may give you rights including the right to:

  • ask what personal information is held about you;
  • ask for inaccurate information to be corrected;
  • ask for information to be deleted in certain circumstances;
  • ask for processing to be restricted;
  • object to certain uses of your information;
  • receive certain information in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

These rights are not absolute and may depend on the reason the information is being held.

To make a privacy request, email: flamingorisecoaching@gmail.com

12. Automated decision-making

FlamingoRise Coaching does not currently make significant decisions about customers using solely automated decision-making or profiling.

13. Marketing

FlamingoRise will not add you to a marketing mailing list simply because you send an enquiry or buy a product unless there is a lawful basis for doing so.

Where marketing consent is requested, you can withdraw that consent at any time.

14. Children

FlamingoRise products and services are not intended to collect personal information directly from children unless this is explicitly stated for a particular service and appropriate safeguards are in place.

If you believe personal information about a child has been provided inappropriately, contact FlamingoRise so the matter can be reviewed.

15. Keeping your information secure

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are used to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no website, email system or online service can guarantee absolute security.

16. Cookies

The FlamingoRise website may use cookies or similar technologies where they are required for site functionality or provided by third-party services.

More detailed information will be available in the FlamingoRise Cookie Notice.

17. Links to other websites

FlamingoRise may link to websites or services operated by third parties.

FlamingoRise is not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites. You should review the privacy information provided by those services separately.

18. Complaints

If you have a concern about how your personal information has been handled, please contact FlamingoRise first so there is an opportunity to investigate and resolve the issue.

Email: flamingorisecoaching@gmail.com

You also have the right to raise a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), the UK's data protection regulator.

Visit: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint

19. Changes to this Privacy Notice

This notice may be updated when FlamingoRise changes the way it operates, introduces new services or when legal requirements change.

The latest version will always be published on this website with the updated date shown near the top of the page.
