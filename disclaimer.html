The important boundary-setting bit

Disclaimer

FlamingoRise is here to help you reflect, explore, learn and figure some shit out. It is not here to diagnose you, treat you, replace professional healthcare or tell you how to live your life.

FlamingoRise Coaching provides coaching, educational resources, self-guided materials and personal development tools.

These are intended for general information, reflection and personal development.

Last updated: 11 September 2026

1. FlamingoRise is not medical or mental health treatment

FlamingoRise Coaching is not a medical service, psychotherapy service, counselling service, psychiatric service or clinical mental health provider.

Nothing on this website, within a workbook, course, downloadable resource, coaching session or other FlamingoRise material should be treated as:

  • medical advice;
  • psychological or psychiatric diagnosis;
  • psychotherapy or counselling;
  • medical treatment;
  • crisis support;
  • a substitute for professional healthcare.

If you have concerns about your physical or mental health, you should seek advice from an appropriately qualified healthcare professional.

2. Resources covering difficult subjects

Some FlamingoRise resources explore subjects including:

  • trauma;
  • grief and loss;
  • difficult or unhealthy relationships;
  • narcissistic relationship dynamics;
  • anger;
  • emotional wellbeing;
  • coping mechanisms;
  • self-worth and confidence;
  • nervous-system regulation;
  • personal growth and behaviour patterns.

These subjects can sometimes bring up difficult thoughts, feelings or memories.

You are responsible for deciding whether a particular resource feels appropriate for you and whether you want additional professional support while working through it.

You are always allowed to stop, pause, skip a section or return to something later.

3. Coaching is not therapy

Coaching focuses on reflection, goals, personal development, perspective, decision-making and moving forward.

Coaching is not the same as counselling, psychotherapy, psychology, psychiatry or medical treatment.

FlamingoRise Coaching does not diagnose mental health conditions or provide treatment for them.

Where your needs fall outside the scope of coaching, you may be encouraged to seek appropriate professional or specialist support.

4. No guaranteed results

Humans are annoyingly individual. What works brilliantly for one person might do absolutely nothing for another.

FlamingoRise does not guarantee that any workbook, exercise, course, coaching session, journaling prompt, meditation, technique or other resource will produce a particular result.

Your results may depend on many things, including your circumstances, choices, experiences, level of participation and the suitability of a particular resource for you.

Testimonials, examples and personal experiences should not be interpreted as guarantees that another person will achieve the same outcome.

5. You make your own decisions

FlamingoRise resources are designed to help you think, reflect and explore options.

They do not make decisions for you.

Any decisions you make about your relationships, work, finances, health, lifestyle, personal development or other areas of your life remain your own responsibility.

Where a decision requires specialist advice, you should speak to an appropriately qualified professional.

6. Not legal, financial or professional advice

FlamingoRise content is not legal, financial, investment, employment or other regulated professional advice.

If a situation requires specialist professional advice, please obtain that advice from someone appropriately qualified to provide it.

7. Spiritual and reflective resources

Some FlamingoRise materials explore spiritual, reflective or personal belief-based practices.

These materials are offered for personal reflection and exploration.

They are not presented as scientifically established medical treatments, guaranteed predictions or factual claims about matters that cannot reasonably be verified.

You are free to take what resonates, question what doesn't and leave the rest.

8. Meditation, breathing and grounding exercises

FlamingoRise may include breathing exercises, grounding techniques, meditation or relaxation practices.

These should be used in a way that feels safe and comfortable for you.

Stop an exercise if you feel unwell, distressed, faint, dizzy or otherwise uncomfortable.

If you have a medical condition or other reason to be unsure whether a technique is suitable for you, speak to an appropriate healthcare professional before using it.

9. Emergency and crisis support

FlamingoRise is not an emergency or crisis service.

If you or somebody else is in immediate danger, call 999 or go to your nearest Accident & Emergency department.

If you need urgent medical help in the UK but it is not an emergency, you can contact NHS 111.

Do not wait for an email, social-media reply, coaching response or FlamingoRise message if you need urgent help.
